5. Fama, E. and K. French (1993). Common risk factors in the returns on stocks and bonds. *JFE*.
6. Fama, E. and K. French (2015). A five-factor asset pricing model. *JFE*.
7. Chui, A., S. Titman and J. Wei (2010). Individualism and momentum around the world. *Journal of Finance*.
8. Liu, J., R. Stambaugh and Y. Yuan (2019). Size and value in China. *JFE*.
